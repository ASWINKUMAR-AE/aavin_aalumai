# Aavin Aalumai Brand Identity & Design Tokens

## Brand Analysis
- **Official Identity**: Aavin Aalumai (ஆவின் ஆளுமை)
- **Primary Emblem**: Circular crest featuring the classic "aavin" blue rectangular emblem with dynamic green curve, bold Tamil lettering "ஆவின் ஆளுமை", flowing white milk splash swirl, and cow-calf pastoral silhouette with green leaves.
- **Primary Color**: `#0A73C0` (Aavin Brand Blue)
- **Secondary / Crest Color**: `#054C97` (Aavin Deep Navy Blue)
- **Accent Color**: `#7CB340` (Aavin Spring Leaf Green)
- **Surface / Milk Tone**: `#FFFFFF` (White Milk Splash) / `#F0F7FF`
- **Text Primary**: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
- **Text Secondary**: `#475569` (Slate 600)
- **Status Colors**:
  - Success: `#16834A`
  - Warning: `#D99000`
  - Error: `#C62828`
  - Info: `#0284C7`

## Usage Rules
- Preserve the authentic Aavin crest without distortion.
- Minimum clear space around the emblem is 16px.
- Use high-contrast industrial typography for HMI counter reading displays.
